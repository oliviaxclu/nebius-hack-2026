# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

**Use only the fictional patient on screen. Do not show, name, or describe any real person's records. If you tell a personal story, tell it only from your own family perspective, with that person's permission, with no identifying details and no mention of where anyone was treated.**

| Time | Say | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:25 | "About 18% of people with diabetes have visual impairment. Their devices assume sight: reading a dose window, seeing a drop at a needle tip, noticing a needle has two caps. [One sentence of your own, anonymous, with permission.] Every glucose app says 'high.' None says why, and none tells you the insulin might not be working." | Title card, then the app |
| 0:25–0:50 | "Meet Ray, a fictional patient on insulin, a weekly GLP-1 and a short prednisone course. Thirty days of sensor data, all on his device." | Left panel: day strip, red pattern card |
| 0:50–1:40 | Tap the starter "Why were my mornings so high…". Let it read aloud. "Steroids explain the afternoons. They don't explain high *mornings* every day, even while the log says insulin was taken and the dose was raised. Prime lines up the competing explanations and tells him to check the pen and call his care team. It won't say a dose was missed, and it won't tell him what to take." | Chat answer; click a green citation chip; show an *unverified* chip if one appears |
| 1:40–2:05 | "Because he can't see the needle, there's a spoken pen check." | Pen check dialog: two caps, safety test, counting clicks. "It works with no model at all." |
| 2:05–2:30 | "Everything stays on his device. This panel shows exactly what left it, with his name replaced." | Right panel: placeholders highlighted |
| 2:30–2:50 | "Built on Nemotron through Nebius Token Factory. The numbers come from code, the explanation from the model, and every citation is checked." | Finish on the safe-next-steps card |

Rehearse on the deployed URL from a logged-out browser. Record at 100% zoom with High contrast off, then show it on once.
